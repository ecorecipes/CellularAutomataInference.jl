CellularAutomataInference.jl

Fitting cellular automata to data, for models built with CellularAutomataCore.jl.

Part of the ecorecipes cellular-automata ecosystem: CellularAutomataCore.jl → CellularAutomataInference.jl (fitting), alongside CategoricalCellularAutomata.jl (applied category theory) and EcologicalCellularAutomata.jl (model zoo).

Two routes to a posterior

With a likelihood. When the probability of the data can be written down, sample the posterior by Markov chain Monte Carlo. A PosteriorDensity combines a log-likelihood with priors (a NamedTuple of distributions from Distributions.jl) on an unconstrained scale, as a LogDensityProblems object; wrapped in LogDensityModel, it is sampled through the AbstractMCMC interface by AdaptiveMetropolis (Haario et al., 2001) or by any other sampler of that interface. The core's path_logpdf gives the exact likelihood of a path observed at every step, and its observation models give the probability of what a survey or sensor reports.

Data rarely show every step: surveys are months apart, and an infection is seen only once symptoms appear. The states in between are then latent, and the likelihood sums over them. Data augmentation (Gibson and Renshaw, 1998) samples them instead, alongside the parameters: an AugmentedModel states the joint density of data and latent variables and a move for the latent variables, and DataAugmentation alternates those moves with adaptive Metropolis moves of the parameters.

model = AugmentedModel(loglik, latent_step!, priors, latent0)
chains = sample(rng, model, DataAugmentation(), MCMCThreads(), 20_000, 4)
posterior_summary(chains)

By simulation. When only simulation is practical, abc_smc runs approximate Bayesian computation by sequential Monte Carlo with differential-evolution moves, through ABCdeZ.jl, on a simulator or directly on a cellular automaton problem, and estimates the model evidence for comparing models. The core's abc_rejection remains the simplest alternative.

post = abc_smc(prob, (β=Uniform(0, 1),), distance, observed; tolerance=2, rng=Xoshiro(1))

Checking a fit

effective_sample_size (Geyer, 1992), split rhat (Gelman et al., 2013) and posterior_summary summarise chains and weighted particles. Before fitting real data, fit data simulated from known parameters and check that they are recovered.
